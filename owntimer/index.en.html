A few things you may be wondering.

What happens to recordings I already have?

Existing recordings are kept. If you have more than 5 when updating, that number is protected as your recording limit.

Can I transfer recordings or purchases to a new device?

Recordings and added slots are stored on your device; transfer after reinstalling or changing devices is not guaranteed. Restore Purchase restores your one-time purchase with the same store account, not your recording files.

Where can I learn more about sounds and controls?

Lock-screen behavior and sound volume depend on your OS and device settings. Try a short countdown first. Visit Support for more details.
