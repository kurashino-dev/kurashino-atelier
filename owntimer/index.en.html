OwnTimer

See the time left. Hear your own recording when it is done.

Home · Privacy · Terms · Support | 日本語

OwnTimer combines alarms, timers and a visual timer in one calm app. Watch the water in a glass slowly go down to see how much time is left, and be told when it is done with a recording you chose.

  • Visual timer: see the time left as the amount of water.
  • Alarms: choose a time and weekdays for your daily plans.
  • Recordings and sounds: record a voice or any sound you like (up to 10 seconds) as the notification sound.
  • Presets: save the durations and sounds you use often.
  • Five color themes and 11 languages.

The core features are free, with banner ads on the alarm, regular timer, settings and sound screens, in the visual timer settings, and before or after recording (not on the visual timer water display, while an alert is ringing, during recording or during sound previews). A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase hides ads. There is no account and no cloud sync; recordings and settings stay on your device.
