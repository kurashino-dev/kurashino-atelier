OwnTimer Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 2, 2026

Kurashino ("we", "us") provides OwnTimer, an alarm, timer and visual timer app (the "App"). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Summary

  • No account is required. We run no server for the App, and there is no cloud sync.
  • Your alarms, timers, settings and recordings are stored only on your device.
  • We have not included analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the App.
  • You can optionally send bug reports and feedback through a Google Form. The form runs outside the App, in your browser (see Section 13).
  • While you use the free version, Google AdMob banner ads are shown, and Google's advertising SDK may process some information (see Section 5).
  • If you own "Remove Ads", the App does not initialize the advertising SDK.

2. Information stored on your device

Alarms, timers, presets (your favorite durations and sounds), notification settings, display settings (colors, language and similar), recordings, and whether you own "Remove Ads" are stored only on your device, in the App's storage. We do not receive them.

Whether this data is included in a device backup (iCloud Backup, Android backup and similar) depends on your operating system and its settings. The App itself has no backup or sync feature.

3. Microphone and recordings

With your permission, the microphone is used only when you record a voice or sound to use as a notification sound. Each recording is up to 10 seconds; there is no limit on the number of recordings. You can use the App with the built-in sounds without granting microphone access.

Recordings are stored on your device. The App does not send them to our servers or to advertising services. You can choose to export a recording yourself (on iOS, using the share sheet; export is not currently offered on Android) and decide whether to pass the exported file to another app or person. How that recipient handles it is up to them.

If you record another person's voice, or a sound someone else holds rights to, please obtain their consent or permission (see the Terms of Use).

4. Notifications

Alarm and timer notifications are local notifications scheduled on your device. The App does not use a push notification server or push tokens. You can change the notification permission at any time in your device settings.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

While you use the free version, a Google AdMob banner ad is shown at the end of the alarm list, the regular timer screen and the settings screen. To show ads, Google's advertising SDK (Google Mobile Ads SDK) may process information such as the following for ad delivery, fraud prevention, measurement and quality improvement:

  • IP address and the approximate location that can be inferred from it
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app identifiers and similar)
  • Advertising-related identifiers (for example the advertising ID on Android, and identifiers scoped to the app's publisher on iOS; this varies with your OS and its settings)
  • Ad interaction information, such as impressions and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as ad load results and errors

The information actually processed depends on Google's implementation, your OS and its settings, and your region. Google's handling of information is described by Google:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob data disclosure (iOS) / AdMob data disclosure (Android)

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. It requests non-personalized ads and limits ad content to a calm maximum content rating of G. On iOS, the App does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt. These settings do not mean that no information is sent to Google.

You can review and change ad-related settings in your device's OS settings (for example resetting or deleting the advertising ID on Android, or the Privacy & Security settings on iOS) and in Google Ad Settings.

If the App confirms that you own "Remove Ads", the advertising SDK is not initialized and no ads are shown.

Where required for your region, Google’s consent form lets you make choices about advertising. When applicable, you can change these choices in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Your choices do not restrict the basic alarm and timer functions.

6. In-app purchase

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by the store, and we do not receive them. The App communicates with the store (Apple or Google) to make the purchase, to check whether you own it, and to restore it. The stores' terms and privacy policies apply to purchase and transaction information. We do not obtain your name, email address or payment details through your purchase.

7. What the App does not use

  • Accounts or sign-in, our own servers, or cloud sync
  • Analytics, crash-reporting or behavioral tracking SDKs (other than the advertising SDK)
  • Location, contacts, camera or photo library access
  • Cross-app or cross-site tracking through the iOS tracking permission (ATT)

8. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: you can delete alarms, presets and recordings individually in the App. Uninstalling the App removes its on-device data (except for any copies in OS backups).
  • Our servers: we do not store your data on servers, so there is no App data for us to delete.
  • Ad and purchase information: retained by Google and by the operators of the App Store and Google Play under their own policies. Please use their channels.
  • Contact form responses: kept only as long as needed to investigate and improve the App. Because the form does not ask for your name or contact details, we may not be able to find and delete a specific response.

9. Sharing with third parties

We do not collect your information through the App (other than responses you choose to submit through the contact form), so we do not sell or provide it to third parties. Information handled by the advertising SDK (Google) and by the stores (Apple, Google) is governed by those companies' policies, as described in Sections 5 and 6. That information may be processed in facilities outside your country.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (and we do not collect personal information through the App in the first place). If you are a parent or guardian with a concern, please use the contact form below.

11. Your rights

We do not hold personal information about you through the App. Because the contact form does not ask for your name or contact details, we cannot tell whose response is whose, so we may not be able to act on requests for access, correction or deletion of form responses. For ad and purchase information, please use the channels of Google, Apple and the relevant store.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date. For significant changes, we will also notify you in the App or the store description.

13. Contact form (bugs and feedback)

You can open a Google Form from "Bug reports & feedback" in the App's settings, or from the "Contact form" link on each page of this site. The form opens in your device's browser and runs outside the App.

  • The App does not receive, store or relay what you type in the form, and does not automatically send any App, OS or device information.
  • The form does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or file attachments. Please do not write personal information or other people's information.
  • What you type and submit is processed by Google Forms and handled under Google's privacy policy (Google Privacy Policy).
  • We review responses in the Google Forms dashboard and use them only to investigate and improve the App. The form does not ask for contact details, so we cannot reply to individual responses.
