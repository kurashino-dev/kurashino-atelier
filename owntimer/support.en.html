What to include when you contact us

  • Your device model and OS version
  • The OwnTimer version (shown on the store page)
  • Which screen you were on, what you did, and what happened (and since when, if you know)
  • Purchases and refunds are handled by the store where you bought the App (App Store or Google Play). Please do not write order numbers in the form.

The form does not ask for personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or address, and does not accept attachments such as recordings. Please do not write them.

FAQ

Notifications do not arrive or make no sound

  • Check in your device settings that notifications are allowed for OwnTimer.
  • Check the device volume, silent mode, and Do Not Disturb or Focus settings. A Focus may need to allow notifications from OwnTimer.
  • Battery-saving mode and battery optimization can affect notifications. On Android, also check the "Alarms & reminders" permission and any battery restriction on the app.
  • Sound and vibration after the app is closed or while the device is locked follow the OS and its settings. For important events, please use another method as well.

The microphone does not work or I cannot record

The microphone is used only when you record. In your device settings, open OwnTimer (on Android: Apps → OwnTimer → Permissions) and check that the microphone is allowed. You can use the timer with the built-in sounds without microphone access.

How do I unlock the visual timer's touch lock?

Press and hold the key icon at the top right of the running screen for 2 seconds. The lock only prevents accidental touches inside the app; it does not lock the whole device.

I bought "Remove Ads" but still see ads / ads came back after changing devices

Using the same store account (Apple Account or Google account) you bought with, go to:

Settings → Remove Ads → See details → Restore Purchase

Purchases cannot be transferred between Apple and Google (a purchase on iPhone does not apply on Android, and vice versa). Refunds and purchase management are handled by the store where you bought it (App Store or Google Play).

How do I export a recording?

On iOS, in the sound list tap the "..." on your own recording and choose "Export" to pick where to save or send it in the share sheet (WAV format). Export is not currently offered on Android. If you share another person's voice or a sound someone else holds rights to, please get their consent.

What happens to my data when I change phones?

OwnTimer has no cloud sync or backup feature. Alarms and recordings live on your device and follow your device's backup settings (iCloud Backup, Android backup and similar). On iOS, exporting your recordings to keep them elsewhere is a good idea. "Remove Ads" can be restored with "Restore Purchase" as described above.

About ads

While you use the free version, Google ads are shown above the tab bar on the alarm and regular timer screens (not on the settings or visual timer screens, or while ringing). See the Privacy Policy for how information is handled. If you see an inappropriate ad, please let us know.

About the bundled sounds

"Forest", "Ocean", "Rain" and "Bush warbler" are short edits of recordings released as public domain (CC0), bundled in the App (thanks to the contributors on Freesound and OpenGameArt). "Fur Elise", "Canon" and "Twinkle Twinkle" are original sounds that OwnTimer synthesized from transcribed public-domain melodies. The App does not stream any of them from the internet.

Deleting data

You can delete alarms, presets and recordings individually in the App. Uninstalling the App removes its on-device data. We hold no data about you on any server.
