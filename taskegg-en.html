Turn everyday things to do at home into fun missions.

Shaped by the voices of parents, guardians, and children, TaskEgg turns everyday tasks into missions. Eggs grow with missions and hatch into characters. It aims to encourage initiative while easing some of the burden on those who offer support.

  • Missions: set things to do that suit your household, such as getting ready, brushing teeth, homework and reading.
  • Eggs and characters: as missions are completed, the egg grows and a character hatches. Give characters names and browse them in the Character Book.
  • My Missions: add ideas of your own.
  • Coins and rewards: missions earn in-app coins for rewards your household chooses. Rewards and coins can also be turned off.
  • A room for each family member: create up to 8 rooms. Adults can use it for their own daily tasks too. Adult settings are protected by a PIN.
  • Available in 11 languages.

Downloading TaskEgg and using its core features is free, and there are no ads. An optional one-time Premium purchase adds adding and editing calendar plans, the full Story of the Month, extra backgrounds and missions with dates. There is no automatically renewing subscription. No account is needed, and what you enter is stored on your device.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Core features such as missions, egg growth, character collecting, the Character Book, rewards and coin savings are free.

Are there ads?

No.

Can adults use it?

Yes. Each room has its own missions, and adults can use it for their own daily tasks.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal, and it covers all rooms in the app.
