This page brings together information about TaskEgg and how to get in touch.

Bug reports, feedback and requests

You can report a problem with TaskEgg, or send feedback and requests, using the dedicated form. No name or email address is required.

Send a bug report or feedback

This form is for receiving messages only, and we do not reply to submissions individually. We read what you send and use it to improve the app. You are welcome to write in your own language.

Inquiries that need a reply

If you need a reply — for example to check the status of a purchase, or to ask about how data is handled — please use the inquiry form.

Purchases and refunds

TaskEgg's Premium features are a one-time purchase and do not renew automatically. There is nothing to cancel.

Purchases and refunds are handled under the terms and policies of the App Store or Google Play where you made the purchase. Please see the Terms of Service for details.
