Using OwnMetronom

OwnMetronom is a practice tool. Pitch readings depend on the instrument, microphone and surroundings. Please allow microphone access only when using the tuner or recording.

Recordings and sharing

Only record and share material you have the right or permission to use. Recordings stay on your device; keep copies of recordings you want to retain.

Free slots and purchases

The free plan has 3 reusable recording slots. Each earned optional-ad reward adds one slot without expiry in this app on this device; restoration after uninstall or device replacement is not guaranteed. The one-time purchase removes ads and the recording-count limit, subject to available device storage. Store terms apply to payment, restoration and refunds.
